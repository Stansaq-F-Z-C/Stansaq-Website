Polyurethane Systems

Cast, dispersion and coating-grade polyurethane raw materials for elastomer, foam and surface-finishing applications.

Applications

  • Cast elastomer components
  • Coating and lamination formulations
  • Adhesive and sealant systems

Industries

  • Rubber & elastomer processing
  • Automotive components
  • Construction

Benefits

  • Consistent batch-to-batch material data
  • Technical formulation support from the manufacturer
  • Local stock reduces lead time on standard grades
02

Industrial Films

Extruded technical films for protective, functional and structural use across manufacturing and construction.

Applications

  • Surface protection during fabrication
  • Vapour and moisture barriers
  • Release and process films

Industries

  • Construction
  • Industrial manufacturing
  • Packaging

Benefits

  • Consistent gauge and optical clarity
  • Range of polymer chemistries from one source
  • Custom widths and roll lengths on request
03

Medical Films

Films manufactured and documented to medical packaging and device-contact requirements.

Applications

  • Sterile barrier packaging
  • Device-contact and wound-care substrates
  • Pouch and blister laminate components

Industries

  • Medical
  • Packaging

Benefits

  • Full traceability documentation per batch
  • Compliance with relevant medical packaging standards
  • Consistent seal performance across production runs
04

Protective Films

Temporary protective films for surfaces during fabrication, transport, storage and installation.

Applications

  • Metal and glass surface protection
  • Transport and installation masking
  • Anti-scratch overlays for finished components

Industries

  • Automotive
  • Construction
  • Industrial manufacturing

Benefits

  • Clean removal without adhesive residue
  • UV-stable grades for extended outdoor exposure
  • Custom adhesion levels by substrate
05

Pipe Relining

Cured-in-place liner systems for the trenchless rehabilitation of buried pipe infrastructure.

Applications

  • Sewer and stormwater pipe rehabilitation
  • Potable water main relining
  • Industrial pipe rehabilitation without excavation

Industries

  • Infrastructure
  • Pipe rehabilitation
  • Construction

Benefits

  • Avoids open-cut excavation and surface disruption
  • Restores structural and hydraulic performance
  • Installation typically completed within a single shift per section

Industries

  • Rubber & elastomer processing
  • Industrial manufacturing

Benefits

  • Spare parts and service support in-region
  • Line specification matched to local power and utility conditions
