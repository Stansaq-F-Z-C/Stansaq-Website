About Stansaq

A representation business built on technical credibility, not transaction volume.

Stansaq develops and manages market access for a small number of manufacturing partners across the polymer, elastomer and industrial film sectors, operating from Sharjah with reach across the wider Middle East.

Mission

Give specialist manufacturers a technically credible route into Middle East markets.

Most of our principals manufacture materials or equipment that require specification support, not just a price list. Our work is to carry that technical understanding into the region, sit with end users through qualification and trials, and keep supply running once a relationship is established.

Vision

A durable bridge between European manufacturing and regional industry.

We aim to be the point of continuity between a small group of manufacturers and the industries that depend on their materials — construction, infrastructure, packaging, rubber and medical converting — as those markets grow and their compliance requirements tighten.

§ 01

Company story

How the business is structured and where it operates.

Stansaq is registered in the Sharjah Airport International Free Zone and operates as a principal representation and market-development company. Rather than holding broad, undifferentiated stock, Stansaq works on a partner-by-partner basis: each principal is represented under a defined mandate covering specific product lines, applications and, in most cases, exclusivity within agreed territory.

This structure means technical staff working on a given material or machine line have direct visibility of that manufacturer's specifications, quality documentation and application data — rather than relying on secondhand product sheets. It also means the portfolio grows deliberately: new principals are added where there is a genuine gap in regional representation, not to broaden a catalogue.

§ 02

Founder

Leadership background across polymer manufacturing and Middle East market development.

Portrait — S.T. Quadri

Syed T. Quadri

Founder

Syed T. Quadri founded Stansaq after more than 25 years working across polymers, elastomers and industrial market development in the Middle East. His career has spanned technical roles in polymer and elastomer manufacturing supply, and commercial roles building distribution and representation structures for materials manufacturers entering the region.

Across this period he has worked closely with rubber processors, film converters, construction contractors and infrastructure operators, developing a working understanding of how specification, import regulation and end-user qualification processes differ across Gulf and wider Middle East markets — and where manufacturers most often lose time or credibility in the process.

Stansaq was established to formalise that experience into a dedicated representation structure: one that gives manufacturing partners a single, technically literate point of contact for the region, rather than a loose network of agents.
