Industries

Eight sectors, each with its own specification and compliance path.

The same material or machine line is qualified differently depending on the sector it enters. Our technical staff work within each sector's own standards and procurement process.

01

Rubber

Compounders and processors using cast polyurethane systems and processing equipment alongside conventional rubber.

02

Construction

Contractors and fabricators specifying protective films, sealants and PU-based construction materials.

03

Medical

Medical device and packaging manufacturers requiring documented, compliant film substrates.

04

Packaging

Flexible packaging converters sourcing barrier films and laminate components.

05

Infrastructure

Utilities and contractors managing water, wastewater and industrial pipe networks.

06

Pipe Rehabilitation

Specialist contractors delivering trenchless relining programmes for buried infrastructure.

07

Industrial Manufacturing

General manufacturers sourcing films, protective materials and processing equipment.

08

Automotive

Component manufacturers using cast elastomers and protective films in production.
